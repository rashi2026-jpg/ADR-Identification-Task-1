# Task 1 – ADR Identification Basics

## Patient Scenario

A 45-year-old female patient was started on Amoxicillin. The patient developed rash, itching, nausea, and dizziness approximately 3 days after starting the medication. The medication had been started 5 days ago.

## ADR Identification

 1. Is this an ADR?

Yes. This case represents a potential suspected Adverse Drug Reaction (ADR).

 2. Suspected Drug

Amoxicillin

 3. Reported Adverse Reactions

- Rash
- Itching
- Nausea
- Dizziness

 4. Time to Onset

The symptoms appeared approximately 3 days after starting Amoxicillin.

 5. Reason for Suspecting Amoxicillin

There is a temporal relationship between the use of Amoxicillin and the onset of the reported symptoms. The symptoms developed after the medication was started, therefore Amoxicillin is considered the suspected drug.

## Conclusion

The patient experienced rash, itching, nausea, and dizziness after starting Amoxicillin. Based on the available information, this is considered a potential suspected ADR associated with Amoxicillin. Further clinical information and assessment would be required to establish the causality of the reaction.
